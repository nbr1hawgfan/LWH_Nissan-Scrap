How these numbers work

Scrapped vs received
Units scrapped during the range divided by units received during the range. Scrapped units may have been received earlier.
Received-unit scrap rate
Of the units received during the range, the share that has gone to scrap so far. Recent ranges keep rising as units are dispositioned, so ranges ending in the last 21 days are marked as still developing.
Scrap share of inventory
Units scrapped during the range divided by units available: on hand at the start plus units received during the range.
Daily averages
Totals divided by operating days: days in the range with any receiving or scrap activity.
Scrap includes
SCRAP OUT loads and scrap transfers referenced as scrap. Engines and transmissions only; packaging and other items are excluded.

Weekly (Sunday to Saturday)

Cohort scrap % follows the units received that week. Weeks that ended less than 21 days ago are still developing.
